Design Thinking Stages in Space Systems

How engineers design reliable systems for extreme environments.

1. Empathize

Understand the needs of scientists, mission operators, astronauts and engineers: reliability, clarity, speed and zero-error workflows.

2. Define

Frame problems clearly: “Mission teams need one dashboard to monitor satellite health in real time.”

3. Ideate

Create solutions like autonomous alerts, rover control UIs, space weather analytics, digital twins and fail-safe communication tools.

4. Prototype

Build control room dashboards, simulation software, telemetry apps, operator consoles and mission planning systems.

5. Test

Run simulations, stress tests and scenario drills. Engineers improve usability, resilience and safety continuously.
